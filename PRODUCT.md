# Seruendesh

## Platform

ios

## Product Purpose

Help a person choose places in Astana and make a walk from a starting point, available time and preferences. Conversation and photo attachments support discovery; the map and ordered stops make the result usable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing SwiftUI iOS26 app; native MapKit, three tabs: walks, events, saved places.
- Local data by default, with an explicit optional backend mode. Preserve photos, session restoration, Journal, favorites, account behavior, date filters and provider links.
- Routes and events cannot be saved as favorites. Places can be saved.
- Local event examples link honestly to city listings on Sxodim/Ticketon; supplied real event links retain their exact destination.
- Estimated local walking geometry remains labelled as approximate; no invented GPS or network routing.
- Keep the private pet_project recovery repository and main branch; no new branches in other repositories.

## Brand Commitments

Seru mascot and Russian conversational voice. Green remains an accent. The user approved a quieter city-guide direction with neutral grounds, credible photography and fewer containers after rejecting the previous look as too AI-like.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable layouts with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver actions,44pt minimum controls and Reduce Motion support. Keep native iOS navigation expectations.
