## Selection and mapping

- `results-asset-001` and `saved-asset-000` show the same confirmed Astana Botanical Garden alley, photographed on 16 August 2024. The previous saved thumbnail showed the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation pyramid, which does not identify the botanical garden.
- `results-asset-002` and `saved-asset-001` show Nurjol Boulevard from the Bayterek observation tower. This replaces generic café scenes with an identifiable view of the named boulevard; it is not a photograph of a particular café.
- `results-asset-003` shows the fountain in Lovers Park, with the Commons camera coordinates close to the app’s verified park location. It is not presented as a new photograph of a specific event or season.
- `plan-asset-001` shows the square beside Bayterek rather than a close-up of the monument. A portrait of the monument was rejected because it cropped poorly in landscape and the Commons monument category notes a freedom-of-panorama concern. The selected square photograph does not feature the monument itself.
- `saved-asset-002` remains mapped exclusively to the Ishim river place. It now shows a confirmed view of the river and bridge, photographed on 6 July 2024.

The jazz cover is an archival photograph of a 1960 Rotterdam performance, not the fictional Astana summer concert. The museum cover depicts historic public-domain artworks in Copenhagen, not the fictional Steppe Art exhibition. The night sky was photographed in California on 14 April 2024, not at the Astana Botanical Garden. The NASA image contains no NASA logo or identifiable person and is credited to NASA / Preston Dyches; its use implies no NASA endorsement.

The old jazz image had malformed festival lettering; the astronomy cover depicted an unverified crowd and setting. They were replaced to avoid invented-looking show documentation. The original images have no repository provenance, so appearance alone cannot establish whether they were generated.
